# Privacy policy — Personal rclone backup

This application is for personal use by its owner.

It accesses Google Drive to upload, download, and manage files
selected by the owner. Files are transferred between the owner's
computer and Google Drive.

The application does not sell Google user data or share it with
advertising or analytics services.

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local rclone
configuration. Access can be revoked through the owner's
Google Account permissions.

This website hosts only information about the application.
It does not receive the application's OAuth credentials or Drive files.
